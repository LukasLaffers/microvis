Homogeneous and Homothetic

How to read this

Homothetic: every isoquant is a radial blow-up of one shape, so the MRTS is the same all along a ray. The shape does not say how much an isoquant produces: any increasing can number them. Homogeneous of degree also fixes the numbers: .

  • Switch between Homogeneous and Homothetic: the isoquants stay where they are, only their outputs change.
  • With Homogeneous, move : the same curves again, and the output at is always times the output at .
  • With Homothetic, change : the outputs follow no power of .
  • Neither: the tangents along a ray turn, so the isoquants are not blow-ups of one shape.
  • Draw the isoquants at equal output steps: their spacing shows the returns to scale.
