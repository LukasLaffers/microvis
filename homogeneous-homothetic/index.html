How to read this

The isoquants are drawn at equal output steps: Along each coloured ray the input mix is fixed and only the scale changes.

Homogeneous of degree : the isoquants are radial blow-ups of each other. Along every ray the tangents are parallel (the MRTS is the same), and the spacing of the isoquants shows the returns to scale: equal spacing for , bunching up for , spreading out for .

Homothetic: the same isoquant shapes with parallel tangents along rays, but the labels follow any increasing : the spacing can change as you move out. Every homogeneous function is homothetic, not the other way round. Neither: the tangents along a ray turn.
