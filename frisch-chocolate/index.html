Frisch's Chocolate Data (Freia, 1935)

How to read this

In 1935 Ragnar Frisch recorded how many kilos of nut chocolate the Freia factory got from two inputs: , moulding and cooling work, and , cocoa fat, both valued in kroner. Each cell of the table is one point of a real production function.

  • Click a cell. Along a row only cocoa fat changes, so the differences are marginal products.
  • Cells on the same ray from the origin show the returns to scale.
  • The isoquant plot redraws Frisch's own figure.

Kilos of nut chocolate

Rows: work (kr). Columns: cocoa fat (kr). Source: Frisch (1935), Table 5a.8, as reproduced in the notes.
