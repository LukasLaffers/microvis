From Firms to Market Supply

Market supply is the horizontal sum of the firms' supply curves, at every price. With fixed costs the sum can jump over the demand curve.

How to read this

Each firm has cost for and : by not producing it avoids . So it supplies nothing until the price reaches its minimum average cost , then jumps to and climbs its marginal cost curve. Market supply adds the outputs at each price.

  • Press Fixed costs, same MC: if demand passes through a jump, no price clears the market.
  • With many identical firms, raise their number : the average firm's supply fills the gap.
