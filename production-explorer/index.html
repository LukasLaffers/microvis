How to read this

The 3D surface is the production function , a hill over the input plane. Its contour lines are the isoquants. Substitution means walking along one contour line: output stays at while the input mix changes. The slope of the isoquant is , and measures how strongly the input mix reacts to the (a large means an isoquant with little curvature). Scale means walking straight up the hill along a ray from the origin: all inputs grow by the same factor . The elasticity of scale says by how many percent output grows when all inputs grow by 1%. One input walks parallel to an axis: only changes, and the slope is the marginal product .

Every technology here is with homogeneous of degree one: shapes the isoquants (substitution), labels them (scale). With the function is homogeneous and everywhere; the other choices are homothetic, and changes along the ray.
