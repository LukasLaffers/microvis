Zoom Out: Netputs Add Up

How to read this

A production plan is a netput vector: outputs count positive, inputs negative. Zoom out and the plans add up: firms to a town, towns to a country, countries to the economy. A good that one actor makes and another uses appears once with + and once with −, so it cancels in the sum.

  • Zoom into a town: its firms and the goods they pass to each other.
  • Zoom out: each town, then each country, is one actor with one netput vector.
  • Pigs cancel inside each country. Potatoes cross the border: where do they cancel, and what is left?
  • Scale the pig farm in Riverton to 0.5: what does Riverton now need from outside?
