How to read this

One technology with two inputs (labour, land) and two outputs (sausages, potatoes). Each figure shows the frontier for one pair of goods, with the other two held fixed at the plan . In every figure the slope is ; whether and are inputs or outputs decides what it is called.

  • Drag the red point along a curve. On the isoquant (top left) the outputs stay the same; on the frontier of outputs (bottom left) the inputs do.
  • The bottom right is always 1 over the top right.
  • Switch on "inputs as z = −q": the top right becomes a production function, as in lecture 1.
