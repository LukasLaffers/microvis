The price of good 1 falls from to . How much better off is the consumer, in money? CV: how much we could take away after the change and leave her as well off as before (utility ). EV: how much we would have to give her before the change to make her as well off as after (). Both are areas to the left of a Hicksian demand curve; the old measure, the change in consumer surplus, is the area to the left of the Marshallian curve, which lies between them.
