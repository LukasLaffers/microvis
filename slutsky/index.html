Substitution and Income Effects

How to read this

Start at and change the price of good 1. First keep the consumer on her indifference curve and change only the relative price: she moves to , the substitution effect, always towards the good that got cheaper. Then adjust her real income: she moves to , the income effect.

  • For a normal good both effects point the same way.
  • Press Giffen good: good 1 is so inferior that the income effect wins, and she buys less of it when it gets cheaper.
