How to read this

Two firms use energy and capital in fixed proportions: they cannot substitute one for the other. When energy gets dearer, the energy-intensive firm's product gets dearer too, consumers switch to the other product, and output moves to the capital-intensive firm. Each firm slides along its own ray, but the industry total turns towards capital. Someone looking only at aggregate data would see substitution that does not happen inside any firm: it is a change in composition.
