How to read this

At the point two directions matter. Along the red ray all inputs grow in proportion: the elasticity of scale says by how many percent output grows when all inputs grow by 1%. Along the blue isoquant output stays the same and the input mix changes: the elasticity of substitution says by how many percent changes when the MRTS changes by 1%.

Both are local: drag around and watch them change, or shade the plane by one of them. The buttons below jump to technologies where both are constant everywhere.
