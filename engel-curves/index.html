Income Expansion Paths and Engel Curves

How to read this

Fix the prices and raise income: the budget line shifts out, and the best bundle moves along the income expansion path (orange). Demand for a good as a function of income is its Engel curve.

  • A: the path is a ray. B: good 2 is a luxury. C: good 1 is inferior.
  • D: the path curves, and the income elasticities change with income.
  • E: good 1 is normal at low incomes and inferior at high ones; its Engel curve rises, then falls.
