Income Expansion Paths and Engel Curves

← All Microvis tools
How to read this

Keep the prices fixed and give the consumer more income: the budget line shifts out in parallel and the best bundle moves along the income expansion path (orange). The demand for each good as a function of income is its Engel curve. In A the path is a ray: income does not change the ratio of the goods. In B good 2 is a luxury, so the consumer buys relatively more of it as she gets richer. In C good 1 is inferior: she buys less of it. In A to C the paths are straight lines; they need not be. In D the path curves: one good is a luxury, the other a necessity, and the income elasticities change with income. In E good 1 is normal at low incomes and inferior at high ones: its Engel curve rises, then falls.
