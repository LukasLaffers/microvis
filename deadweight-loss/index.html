The Deadweight Loss of a Tax

How to read this

A tax raises the price of good 1 from to . The government collects the yellow rectangle ; the consumer loses , the area to the left of the Hicksian demand curve between the two prices. The difference is lost to everybody: the deadweight loss.

  • Raise the tax: the loss grows with the square of the tax.
  • The loss exists because the Hicksian curve slopes down, for normal and inferior goods alike.
