Each firm takes the price as given, so its average and marginal revenue are both and it produces where . While is above average cost the firm makes a profit (green rectangle). That attracts entrants: each new firm adds supply and pushes the market price down. Entry stops when the next firm would make a loss. In a large market that happens close to the bottom of the average cost curve: zero profit in the long run.
